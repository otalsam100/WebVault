What types of businesses can get a website?

WebVault builds websites for a range of small businesses, including restaurants, contractors, salons, fitness businesses, professional services and retailers.

Are WebVault websites mobile-friendly?

Yes. Websites are designed to work on phones, tablets and desktop screens, with clear ways for visitors to call, find your business or request information.

No website designer can guarantee a first-place Google ranking. WebVault sets up search-friendly foundations such as descriptive page titles, page descriptions, clear headings and a sitemap. Rankings depend on many factors over time.
